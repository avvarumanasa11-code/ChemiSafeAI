Standard Decontamination Steps for Exposed Personnel

STEP 1: STRIP

Gross Removal

Carefully remove all clothing from top down (cuts 85-90% of toxic residue). Seal in double hazardous bags.

STEP 2: WASH

15-Minute Deluge

Copious low-pressure lukewarm water shower. Wash hair and open eyes under clean flow for 15+ minutes.

STEP 3: TRIAGE

Clinical Assessment

Transport immediately to clean medical tent for pulse oximetry, lung auscultation, and humidified oxygen.

Medical Triage Matrix (START Protocol)

RED: Immediate (Priority 1)

Laryngeal spasm, respiratory rate > 30/min, severe cyanosis, capillary refill > 2s. Needs immediate intubation/antidotes.

YELLOW: Delayed (Priority 2)

Moderate coughing, mild dyspnea, skin burns < 20% BSA. Keep under continuous 24-hr watch for delayed pulmonary edema.

GREEN: Minor (Walking Wounded)

Eye/throat irritation only, normal vitals, ambulatory. Wash at eyewash station; provide discharge warnings.

BLACK: Expectant / Deceased

No respiration after simple airway maneuver. Move to dignified isolated morgue station.
